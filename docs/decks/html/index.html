The kitchen

One school kitchen, one shelf, one order too late

Today

A buyer logs into the Valio Aimo webshop and searches the catalog by hand.

A school kitchen must also keep the bans its head chef set. In our rule set they are nuts, peanuts and sesame.

When a product runs short in the middle of the week, a person must notice it, find a substitute that breaks no rule and get it approved. The brief names this case: an allergen-safe substitution under pressure, in a school or hospital kitchen.

Our kitchen is a test case. No real kitchen data exists yet.

For Valio

Of the 8,110 products with no allergen data, 3,974 are not food: equipment, cleaning goods, disposables. The other 4,136 are food, 24% of the catalog. Each one waits for a person to read its label once.

The file is synthetic. How many of these a real kitchen orders in a week is a question for the Valio mentors.
