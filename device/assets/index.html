recognize shelf experimental · observes only, never orders

"Recognize shelf" sends the current picture to a vision model (Kimi K3.1 Pro on Verda, via the laptop): it describes every visible package and proposes chips, energy drink or milk only when the description supports it; anything else stays "unknown". No empty photo or enrolment is needed. The two buttons below the picker feed the separate photo-enrolment pipeline.

    shelf now

    knob

    Turn it to set the spend limit. Press it to report the milk as low. Hold it to check a delivery.

    set up the shelf once, in order

    1. Connect the camera

      Camera and power supply into the dongle first, then the dongle into the board.

    2. Place the boxes

      Press Edit boxes, drag every box by its number tag over its product spot (drag the round corner to resize), then press Save boxes. Nothing is saved until then, and purchasing is paused while you edit.

    3. Take the empty photo

      Take every product off the shelf.

    4. Take the full photo

      Put every product back.

    Setup is done. Take a product away to try it.

    delivery check 60 seconds

    Put the cartons on the shelf and the thermo in the crate, then hold the knob or press the key.
