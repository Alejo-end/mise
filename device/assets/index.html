Set up the shelf

Do these once, in order. The board remembers them.

  1. Connect the camera

    Plug it into the hub on the board. The picture shows on the left.

  2. Place the boxes

    Drag the three boxes over the three product spots.

  3. Take the empty photo

    Take every product off the shelf, then press the button.

  4. Take the full photo

    Put every product back, then press the button.

Setup is done. Take a product away to try it.

Kitchen box

Turn the knob to change the most the agent may spend without asking. Press the knob to report the milk as low. Hold it for 2 seconds to check a delivery.

Delivery check

When a delivery arrives, put the cartons on the shelf and the thermo in the crate. Then hold the knob for 2 seconds, or press the button. The box measures for 60 seconds and checks the delivery against the order.
